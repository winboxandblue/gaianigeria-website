Our mission

Food security built on engineering, not luck.

G&IA NG (Green & Intelligent Agriculture) is a pioneering agricultural and agri-industrial development company transforming food production, rural economies, and sustainable land use across Nigeria and Africa.

We treat a farm as a system, not a plot: advanced agronomy tells us what to grow and how; controlled-environment engineering protects it from climate shocks; renewable energy keeps every process running independent of the grid; and precision water management makes sure nothing is wasted. Together, they deliver high-yield, climate-resilient food production at scale.

Advanced agronomy

Crop and soil science calibrated to local conditions, not imported assumptions.

Controlled-environment engineering

Structures and climate control that shield production from weather volatility.

Renewable energy

Solar-first power systems that keep operations running, on-grid or off.

Precision water management

Sensor-guided irrigation that puts water only where and when it's needed.

How it works

One platform, four disciplines, working in a loop.

Each system feeds the others. Agronomic data shapes irrigation schedules; irrigation performance informs energy load; energy availability shapes what the controlled environment can do next. Nothing runs in isolation.
