Our mission

Food security built on engineering, not luck.

G&IA NG (Green & Intelligent Agriculture) is a pioneering agricultural and agri-industrial development company transforming food production, rural economies, and sustainable land use across Nigeria and Africa.

We treat a farm as a system, not a plot: advanced agronomy tells us what to grow and how; controlled-environment engineering protects it from climate shocks; renewable energy keeps every process running independent of the grid; and precision water management makes sure nothing is wasted. Together, they deliver high-yield, climate-resilient food production at scale.

Advanced agronomy

Crop and soil science calibrated to local conditions, not imported assumptions.

Controlled-environment engineering

Structures and climate control that shield production from weather volatility.

Renewable energy

Solar-first power systems that keep operations running, on-grid or off.

Precision water management

Sensor-guided irrigation that puts water only where and when it's needed.

Inside the estate

The G&IA Nasarawa Estate

A fully integrated agri-industrial city — production, infrastructure, power, and processing designed as one continuous system.

A

Primary Food Production

  • 1,000+ acres of open-field crops — rice, wheat, cassava, maize, legumes, vegetables
  • High-tech greenhouses with automated irrigation, fertigation, and climate control
  • Controlled-environment vertical farms for seedling production and premium crops
  • Aquaculture systems supporting fish farming and nutrient cycling
B

Infrastructure & Water Systems

  • Precision irrigation and water storage — reservoirs, boreholes, canals
  • Soil mapping, land conditioning, and agronomy labs for continuous soil optimisation
C

Renewable Energy & Power Systems

  • On-site solar farms, bladeless wind turbines, and hydrogen fuel-cell power stations
  • Battery energy storage ensuring 24/7 stable power for the entire estate
  • Hydrogen-enabled processing plants and logistics operations
D

Processing & Storage Facilities

  • Rice mill, cassava processing plant, flour plant, drying floors, silos, and cold storage
  • Value-added industries for packaging, sorting, and export processing
  • Modular units designed for rapid scaling
